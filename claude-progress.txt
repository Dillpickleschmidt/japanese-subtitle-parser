**JLPT Level**: N4
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 2

**Pattern**: Expression meaning "hardly possible", "improbable", or "unlikely". Literally translates as "(A) is not bound to be true". Suggests that something being almost 100% true is not the case. Weaker than ないはずだ (bound not to be).

### Pattern Implemented: のように・のような (like/as - with noun)

**JLPT Level**: N4
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 2

**Pattern**: The auxiliary verb ようだ used for making observations and comparisons. States that something is "in the manner/way of (A)". The の particle is required before よう when describing a noun. Can be used in adverbial form (のように) or attributive form (のような).

**Structures tested**: 4 tests covering all structure variants

3. Noun + のように + な-Adjective: 私は鳥のように自由だ (I am free as a bird)
4. Noun + のような + Noun: 最近はタブレットのようなスマホが売られています (Recently, tablet-like smartphones are being sold)
