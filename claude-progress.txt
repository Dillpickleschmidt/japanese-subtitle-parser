### ✅ Pattern Completed: に取って (for / to / concerning)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 1

**Pattern**: に取って (also written に取って with kanji) is a structure used to convey opinions or discuss something from a particular perspective. It combines the case-marking particle に, the verb 取る (to take), and the conjunctive particle て. The literal meaning is "to take up (A)", where に marks the standpoint being highlighted. It translates as "to (A)", "for (A)", or "concerning (A)", similar to から見て (from the perspective of).

**Structures tested**: 4 comprehensive tests covering all variants
1. Noun (pronoun) + に取って: 彼は私に取って親みたいな存在です (He is like a parent to me)
2. Noun (name) + に取って: タケミに取って一番大切な人は誰ですか (Takemi, who is the most important person to you?)
3. Noun + に取って (perspective): 私に取ってあの会社は人を働かせすぎだと思います (To me, I think that company makes people work too much)
4. Noun + に取って (important): 私に取って一番大事な友達はあなたです (You are the most important friend to me)

### ✅ Pattern Completed: に代わって (in place of / on behalf of)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 1

**Pattern**: に代わって is an expression that combines the case-marking particle に, the verb 代わる (to substitute/replace), and the conjunctive particle て (or formal conjunctive form). It conveys that (B) is doing something "in place of (A)" or "on behalf of (A)". The pattern can also mean "to replace" or "to succeed" when indicating permanent substitution.

**Structures tested**: 5 comprehensive tests covering all variants
1. Noun + に代わって (in place of): 妹に代わって、私が買い物に行くことに決まった (It has been decided that I am going to go shopping in place of my sister)
2. Noun + に代わって (on behalf of): 私が彼女に代わって会議に出ます (I will be attending the meeting in place of her)
3. Noun + に代わり (formal conjunctive): タナカさんに代わり、今日は私があなたたちの担当をします (In place of Tanaka-san, today, I am going to be in charge of you guys)
4. Noun + に代わって (replacing/succeeding): 父親に代わって、息子が王になった (Replacing his father, the son became king)
5. Noun + に代わり (being replaced by): 世界中ではファックスに代わりメールが主流になった (All around the world, fax has been replaced by email as the main form of communication)

**Implementation details**:
- Matcher: grammar-lib/src/matchers/n3.rs (nikawatte, lines 6292-6335)
- Tests: grammar-lib/src/tests/n3_patterns.rs (nikawatte_tests module, lines 9060-9130)
- Pattern: grammar-lib/src/patterns.rs (Nikawatte, line 4384)

**Tokenization patterns**:
```
て-form (に代わって):
- Noun (名詞) - any noun including suffixes like さん
- に (助詞/格助詞/一般) - case-marking particle
- 代わっ (動詞/自立, base='代わる', 五段・ラ行, 連用タ接続) - verb in て-form conjunctive
- て (助詞/接続助詞) - conjunctive particle

Formal conjunctive (に代わり):
- Noun (名詞) - any noun including suffixes like さん
- に (助詞/格助詞/一般) - case-marking particle
- 代わり (動詞/自立, base='代わる', 五段・ラ行, 連用形) - verb in formal conjunctive form
```

**Key implementation points**:
1. NiParticleMatcher checks に as 助詞/格助詞
2. KawaruVerbMatcher checks base_form == "代わる" as 動詞
3. TeParticleMatcher checks て as 助詞/接続助詞 (optional)
4. Pattern matches both て-form (代わって) and formal conjunctive (代わり)
5. Uses noun_matcher() which matches any 名詞 including suffixes
6. Optional て particle allows both variants with a single matcher

**Pattern ranges**: Include full construction (Noun + に + 代わって/代わり)
- Example: "妹に代わって" (chars 0-6) - て-form
- Example: "彼女に代わって" (chars 2-9) - て-form
- Example: "さんに代わり" (chars 3-9) - formal conjunctive
- Example: "父親に代わって" (chars 0-7) - て-form
- Example: "ファックスに代わり" (chars 5-14) - formal conjunctive
