**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 1

**Pattern**: によると・によれば expresses "according to (A)" or "going off (A)", where (A) is the source of information being conveyed. The pattern combines the case-marking particle に, the verb よる (to be based on), and either the conjunction particle と or the conditional auxiliary ば. The formal variant によりますと uses the polite auxiliary ます.

**Structures tested**: 5 comprehensive tests covering all variants
