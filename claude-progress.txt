### ✅ Pattern Completed: ふりをする (pretend to be/do)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 3 (in feature_list.json), 1 (in patterns.rs - low priority, not highlighted)

**Pattern**: ふりをする is used to show that someone is "pretending to be (A)", "putting on airs of (A)", or "acting as if (A)". This construction comes from the noun 振り (ふり) meaning "a shake" or "behavior pattern shown on the outside", combined with the particle を and する. It can be thought of as "to give off the impression of (A)".

**Structures tested**: 7 comprehensive tests covering all variants
1. Verb[ている] + ふりをする: 僕は子供の頃、よく寝ているふりをした (When I was a child, I would often pretend to sleep)
2. Verb[た] + ふりをする: この蛇は人等の大きな動物が近づくと死んだふりをするらしい (This snake apparently pretends to be dead when humans approach)
3. Verb[ない] + ふりをする: 彼女は何も知らないふりをして黙っていた (She pretended not to know anything and stayed silent)
4. い-Adjective + ふりをする: 嬉しいふりをする (Pretend to be happy)
5. な-Adjective + な + ふりをする: 元気なふりをしなくていいよ (You don't have to pretend to be happy)
6. Noun + の + ふりをする: カエルのふりをして遊んでいる (Playing while pretending to be a frog)
7. Polite form: 分かっているふりをします (Pretend to understand)

### ✅ Pattern Completed: ばかりに (simply because / just because)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 3 (in feature_list.json), 1 (in patterns.rs - low priority, not highlighted)

**Pattern**: ばかりに is used to express "simply because" or "just because". It combines the adverbial particle ばかり with the case-marking particle に. The pattern means that "even with just (A), (B) is/was able to occur", often highlighting a surprising or negative result. While typically used with negative outcomes, it can also express eagerness/enthusiasm when paired with たい or ほしい.

**Structures tested**: 5 comprehensive tests covering all variants
1. Verb[た] + ばかりに: ボールをキャッチしたばかりに、肩が外れました (Simply because I caught a ball, I dislocated my shoulders)
2. い-Adjective + ばかりに: 体が小さいばかりに、クラスメイトにいじめられた (Simply because I am small, my classmates bullied me)
3. な-Adjective + な + ばかりに: 彼は練習が大変なばかりに、部活をやめることにした (Simply because practice was difficult, he decided to quit)
4. Noun + である + ばかりに: 新人であるばかりに、先輩たちがやりたくない仕事を押し付けられた (Simply because I am new, my senpai made me do all the work they didn't want to)
5. Eagerness variant (Verb + たい + ばかりに): 釣りに行きたいばかりに、仕事を休んで海に行った (Simply because he wanted to go fish, he took a day off)

### ✅ Pattern Completed: ばかりでなく (not only...but also)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 3 (in feature_list.json), 1 (in patterns.rs - low priority, not highlighted)

**Pattern**: ばかりでなく is a combination of the adverbial particle ばかり with the conjugated (and negated) form of the auxiliary verb だ. It translates as "not only (A), (B)" or "not limited to (A), (B)". This is the more formal version of だけでなく and appears less frequently in everyday speech.

**Structures tested**: 8 comprehensive tests covering all variants
1. Verb + ばかりではなく: 彼は仕事ばかりではなく、家事もちゃんとやっています

3. な-Adjective + な + ばかりではなく: この池は綺麗なばかりではなく、ワニが沢山いるので危ないです
4. Noun + ばかりではなく: 彼は宝くじに当たって、車ばかりではなく、ボートとバイクも買った
5. Alternative form ばかりか: 彼女は英語ばかりか、中国語も話せる
6. Variation でなく (without は): この問題は難しいばかりでなく、時間もかかる
7. Variation じゃなく (casual): あいつは頭がいいばかりじゃなく、運動もできる

**Pattern**: ばかりだ is used to show that (A) is the only thing that is occurring, or that something "keeps on" doing (A). The pattern combines the adverbial particle ばかり with the auxiliary verbs だ, です, or で (the conjunctive form of だ). It emphasizes that the action or state continues in a single direction, often with negative trends.

**Structures tested**: 4 comprehensive tests covering all variants

3. Verb[る] + ばかりです (polite): 日本の人口は減るばかりです (Japan's population continues to drop)
4. Negative trend variant: 最近は全然体を鍛えていないから、筋肉がなくなっていくばかりだ (I keep on getting less muscle)
