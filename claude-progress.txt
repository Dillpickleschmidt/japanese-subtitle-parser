### ✅ Pattern Completed: に対して (toward / in regard to / in contrast to)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 3

**Pattern**: に対して and に対する are grammar patterns used to show opposition, contrast, or targeting. The verb 対する means "to oppose", but in most contexts it simply means "toward" in the sense of targeting something. に対して creates a pause before the second clause, while に対する directly modifies a following noun.

**Structures tested**: 7 comprehensive tests covering all variants
1. Noun + に対する + Noun (feelings): 君にたいする気持ちは一生変わらない (My feelings toward you will never change)
2. Noun + に対する + Noun (attitude): 私は店員にたいする態度が悪い人が苦手です (I don't like people who have a bad attitude towards store employees)
3. Noun + に対して (speech): ああいう風に、先輩にたいしてタメ口を使うのは良くないと思うよ (I don't think it is best to talk to a senpai using casual speech in that manner)
4. Noun + に対して (mean words): 何でいつも私にたいしてそういう酷いことを言うの？ (Why do you always say mean things like that to me?)
5. Adjective + の + に対して (contrast): 私はサッカーが好きなのにたいして、彼はバドミントンが好き (In contrast to me liking soccer, he likes badminton)
6. Verb + の + に対して (contrast): マサミは難しい本を読んだのにたいして、カスミは簡単な本を読んだ (Masami read a difficult book, whereas Kasumi read an easy book)
7. Noun + の + に対して (contrast): 彼の仕事は楽なのにたいして、僕の仕事はものすごく大変 (His job is carefree, whereas my job is very difficult)

### ✅ Pattern Completed: に合わせて・に合った (in accordance with / matching)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 3

**Pattern**: に合わせて・に合った is a construction using the verb 合わせる (to match/fit) or 合う (to fit/match). It has two main forms:
1. に合わせて - "in accordance with", "matching", "at the same time as"
2. に合った - "that matched", "that fit with"

The pattern combines a noun with the particle に, followed by the verb 合わせる/合う in different conjugations.

**Structures tested**: 6 comprehensive tests covering both variants

4. Noun + に合った + Noun (wallpaper): 壁紙にあった家具が欲しい (I want furniture that matches the wallpaper)
5. Noun + に合わせて (rhythm): リズムに合わせて歌を歌う (I sing while matching the rhythm)
6. Noun + に合わせて (signal): 合図に合わせてスタートする (To start in accordance with the signal)

### ✅ Pattern Completed: に取って (for / to / concerning)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 1

**Pattern**: に取って (also written に取って with kanji) is a structure used to convey opinions or discuss something from a particular perspective. It combines the case-marking particle に, the verb 取る (to take), and the conjunctive particle て. The literal meaning is "to take up (A)", where に marks the standpoint being highlighted. It translates as "to (A)", "for (A)", or "concerning (A)", similar to から見て (from the perspective of).

**Structures tested**: 4 comprehensive tests covering all variants

2. Noun (name) + に取って: タケミに取って一番大切な人は誰ですか (Takemi, who is the most important person to you?)
3. Noun + に取って (perspective): 私に取ってあの会社は人を働かせすぎだと思います (To me, I think that company makes people work too much)
4. Noun + に取って (important): 私に取って一番大事な友達はあなたです (You are the most important friend to me)

### ✅ Pattern Completed: に代わって (in place of / on behalf of)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 1

**Pattern**: に代わって is an expression that combines the case-marking particle に, the verb 代わる (to substitute/replace), and the conjunctive particle て (or formal conjunctive form). It conveys that (B) is doing something "in place of (A)" or "on behalf of (A)". The pattern can also mean "to replace" or "to succeed" when indicating permanent substitution.

**Structures tested**: 5 comprehensive tests covering all variants
1. Noun + に代わって (in place of): 妹に代わって、私が買い物に行くことに決まった (It has been decided that I am going to go shopping in place of my sister)
2. Noun + に代わって (on behalf of): 私が彼女に代わって会議に出ます (I will be attending the meeting in place of her)
3. Noun + に代わり (formal conjunctive): タナカさんに代わり、今日は私があなたたちの担当をします (In place of Tanaka-san, today, I am going to be in charge of you guys)
4. Noun + に代わって (replacing/succeeding): 父親に代わって、息子が王になった (Replacing his father, the son became king)
5. Noun + に代わり (being replaced by): 世界中ではファックスに代わりメールが主流になった (All around the world, fax has been replaced by email as the main form of communication)

