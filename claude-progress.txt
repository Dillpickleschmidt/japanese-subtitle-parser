  - polite[0]: Verb[て] + おきます
  - polite[1]: Verb[て] + ときます (casual contraction)
- **Tokenization**:
  - Standard: Verb (連用形/連用タ接続) + て/で (助詞/接続助詞) + おく (動詞/非自立, base=おく)

  - Optional MasuFormMatcher: Matches ます for polite form
- **Meaning**: "do something in advance", "leave something as is", "do and leave until later"
- **Pattern priority**: 2 (N4 construction)
- **Pattern ranges**:
  - 洗っておく (chars 7-12) - wash in advance
  - 入れておきます (chars 9-16) - will put in advance (polite)
  - 置いとく (chars 10-14) - leave it (casual contraction)
  - 汲んでおく (chars 5-10) - fill up in advance (で-form)
- **Test coverage**:
  - Standard: 寝る前に食器を洗っておく
  - Polite: あなたの財布を鞄に入れておきます
  - Casual contraction: 机の上にパスポートを置いとくね
  - で-form: 水筒に水を汲んでおく

### Implementation Notes
- Key insight: The とく contraction does NOT have a て particle - it goes directly from verb to とく
- Made て/で particle optional to handle both standard (Verb + て + おく) and contraction (Verb + とく) forms
- Both おく and とく tokenize as 動詞/非自立 with their respective base forms
- Pattern confidence ranges from 2.0-3.0 depending on form
