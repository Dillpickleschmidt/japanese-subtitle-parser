### Pattern Implemented: ～のだろうか (I wonder if...)

**JLPT Level**: N4
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 1 (Low - speculation/wondering expression)

**Grammar Description**:
Expresses wondering or speculation about something, equivalent to "I wonder if..." in English. Uses the nominalizer の (or ん as contraction) + だろう/でしょう + か. For な-adjectives and nouns, な is added before の.
