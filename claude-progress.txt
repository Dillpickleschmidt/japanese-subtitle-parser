### ✅ Pattern Completed: わけだ (so/no wonder/that's why - logical conclusion)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 1 (low priority, not highlighted in overlay)

**Pattern**: わけだ is one of the most misunderstood words in Japanese. わけ (訳) means "reason", "cause", "meaning", or "conclusion", but functions similarly to the English word "so". The pattern わけだ highlights the plain existence/determination of something, often translated as "for the reason of (A)", "no wonder it's (A)", or "it's (A), as you'd expect". It simply means "it is so that it's (A)" or "so it's (A)".

**Structures tested**: 5 comprehensive tests covering all major variants
1. Verb + という + わけだ: だから珍しく遅れて来たというわけだ (So that's why you came in late) - chars 11-17
2. い-Adjective + わけだ: どうりで一年たっても下手なわけだ (No wonder you haven't improved) - chars 13-16
3. な-Adjective + な + わけだ: だから定休日なわけだ (So that's why they are closed) - chars 7-10
4. Noun + の + わけだ: あなたは学生のわけだね (So you're a student, right?) - chars 7-10
5. Verb + という + わけです (polite): だから指を動かしたら痛かったというわけですね (So that's why it hurt) - chars 14-21

### ✅ Pattern Completed: もの・もん (because / 'cause - sentence-ending particle)

**JLPT Level**: N3
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 3

**Pattern**: もの is a common noun meaning "thing" that can also function as a sentence-ending particle. When used as a particle, it adds emotion to a statement and presents it as a reason, similar to "because" or "'cause" in English. The literal meaning is "it's an (A) thing!". もの is quite casual and should be avoided in formal situations. It can be shortened to もん for a more casual/childish tone.

**Structures tested**: 8 comprehensive tests covering major variants
1. Verb + もの: だって、あなたはいつも嘘をつくもの → つくもの (13-17)
2. な-Adj + だもの: これはあなたにあげられない。大切だもの → 大切だもの (14-19)
3. Noun + だもの: すぐ泣くに決まってるじゃん、まだこどもだもの → こどもだもの (16-22)
4. Verb + んだもの (emphasized): だって、暑くて寝れないんだもの → ないんだもの (9-15)
5. Noun + なんだもの (emphasized): この子は失敗するに決まってるでしょう、まだ新人なんだもの → なんだもの (23-28)
6. Noun + だもん (casual): もうお腹いっぱいだもん → いっぱいだもん (4-11)
7. Verb + もん (casual): いいよ、お前とはもう遊ばないもん！ → ないもん (12-16)
8. Verb negative + んだもの: 暑くて寝れないんだもの → ないんだもの (5-11)

**Tokenization Notes**:
