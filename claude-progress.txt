**Tokenization Discovery**:
- Kanji form: 気がつく is a single verb token with base_form="気がつく"
- Hiragana form: きがつく splits into き (come) + が + つく (attach)
- The expression literally means "attention/spirit comes and attaches"
- の can be used as nominalizer (名詞/非自立) but のに as single particle (接続助詞) must be avoided
- も particle can intervene: に + も + 気がつく pattern

**Linguistic Note**: This expression preserves the literal meaning of "attention becomes attached to something". The に particle marks the destination/location where 気 (presence of mind/attention) attaches itself, making it a location-focused expression rather than just a state-of-being verb.
