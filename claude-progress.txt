**Tokenization Notes**:
1. **もん has two tokenizations**: As a particle (助詞/終助詞) when directly after verbs/adjectives, or as a noun (名詞/非自立/一般) when after だ
2. **Pattern matches from auxiliary verbs**: In negative verb forms like `遊ばないもん`, the pattern matches from the auxiliary `ない` (助動詞) rather than from the verb stem `遊ば` (動詞). This is a tokenization limitation - ideally we'd capture the full verb phrase, but detecting where verb phrases start is complex.
3. **Content word scope**: The matcher includes auxiliary verbs (助動詞) in ContentWordMatcher to handle patterns like `ないんだもの` and `ないもん`, where ない is an auxiliary that can precede もの/もん.
