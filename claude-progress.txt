**Implementation**: Matcher in n2.rs (lines 1528-1577), tests in n2_patterns.rs (lines 3920-3961)

**Key notes**:

- Dictionary form にそう is UNDETECTABLE due to tokenization issue

- In dictionary form, Kagome misidentifies そう as an adverb instead of the verb

**Tokenization observations**:
- **にそって**: に (助詞/格助詞) + そっ (動詞/自立, base='そう') + て (助詞/接続助詞)

- **にそう** (problematic): に (助詞/格助詞) + そう (副詞/助詞類接続) - NOT detected as verb!

- にそって: ルールにそって (includes noun + に + そっ + て)

**Undetectable case explanation**:
The dictionary form "にそう" creates tokenization ambiguity. In the sentence "希望にそう方向で", Kagome tokenizes "そう" as an adverb (副詞) rather than the verb 沿う. This is because "そう" can be either:
- The verb 沿う (to follow along) - what we want to detect
- The auxiliary そう (seems like, appears) - more common usage

Without conjugation markers (て/た), Kagome defaults to interpreting it as the auxiliary, making the pattern undetectable in dictionary form. Only the conjugated forms にそって and にそった can be reliably detected.
