- する (未然レル接続) = さ (surface)

- Combined with て + いる to express ongoing state of passive
- Pattern automatically extends to include ます in polite form

### Technical Notes

**Passive Quotative Construction Pattern**:
This pattern follows the passive quotative structure:
- Content + と (quotation) + Verb[passive] + ている
- The と marks what precedes it as quoted or considered content
- Passive form indicates the content is something "considered by people in general"
- される (passive of する) emphasizes formal/objective consideration
- ている expresses ongoing state (it's commonly/currently considered)

**Comparison with といわれている**:
- といわれている: "it is said" (emphasizes verbal/hearsay aspect)
- とされている: "it is considered/regarded as" (emphasizes formal judgment/classification)
- Both use same grammatical structure: と + Verb[passive] + ている
