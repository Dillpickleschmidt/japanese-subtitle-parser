#### ていく (N4) ✓
- **File**: grammar-lib/src/matchers/n4.rs (teiku function, lines 152-182)
- **Tests**: 4 tests in n4_patterns.rs (mod teiku_tests, lines 2407-2466)
- **Structures**:
  - standard[0]: Verb[て] + いく
  - polite[0]: Verb[て] + いきます
- **Tokenization**:
  - Verb in 連用形 or 連用タ接続
  - て or で (助詞/接続助詞)

- **Implementation**: 3-token matcher

  - TeDeFormMatcher: Match て/で particle

- **Meaning**: "to go on to", progression away from speaker (time/space)
- **Pattern priority**: 1 (N4 construction)
- **Pattern ranges**: Include full verb + て + いく
  - 食べていく (chars 6-11) - go and eat
  - なっていく (chars 20-25) - will get (better)
  - 持っていった (chars 12-18) - took (past)
  - 頑張っていきます (chars 7-15) - will work hard (polite)
- **Key insight**: Opposite of てくる - progression away from current state
