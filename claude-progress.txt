  - standard[0]: い-Adjective[い] + がる
  - standard[1]: な-Adjective + がる
  - polite[0]: い-Adjective[い] + がります
  - polite[1]: な-Adjective + がります
- **Tokenization patterns**:
  - Dictionary form (compound): 強がる → 強がる (動詞, base_form=強がる) - single token

  - **garu_compound**: Matches verbs where base_form ends with "がる" (強がる, 嫌がる, 怖がり)
    - Single-token matcher for dictionary and conjugated forms
  - **garu_split**: Matches adjective stem + がる suffix (欲しがります, 暑がって)
    - Two-token matcher: Adjective (ガル接続 form) + がる (動詞/接尾)
- **Meaning**: Shows observable signs/behavior of an emotion or state
- **Pattern priority**: 3 (N4 construction)
- **Pattern ranges**:
  - 強がる (chars 12-15) - dictionary form
  - 怖がり (chars 10-13) - 連用形 conjugation
  - 嫌がる (chars 2-5) - dictionary form
  - 欲しがります (chars 15-21) - polite conjugated split form
  - 暑がっ (chars 4-7) - て-form conjugation split form
