  - test_te_iru_contracted (行ってる - contracted)
  - test_te_imasu_polite (食べています - polite full)
  - test_te_masu_contracted_polite (してます - polite contracted)
- **Structures**:
  - Full: Verb[連用形/連用タ接続] + て/で + いる
  - Contracted: Verb[連用タ接続] + てる (single token, NO て particle)
  - Polite full: Verb + て/で + いる[連用形] + ます
  - Polite contracted: Verb + て/で + ます (without いる)
- **Technical notes**:
  - Contracted form `てる` is a single verb token (base_form=てる), NOT て + る
  - Used combined matcher to handle both て particle and てる verb
  - Optional いる matcher allows pattern to match both full and contracted
