### ✅ Pattern Implemented: に気をつける (be careful of, watch out for, pay attention to)

**JLPT Level**: N2
**Category**: Construction
**Priority**: 4 (moderate priority - detected and highlighted)

**Pattern**: に気をつける is a collocation based on 気（き） 'attention/focus', combining the noun 気, を particle, and the verb 付ける 'to attach/adhere'. Used to mark something as requiring attention - "to pay attention to (A)", "to be careful of (A)", "to watch out for (A)".

**Structures tested**: 2 structure variants (2 of 4 from grammar_points_data.json)
1. Noun + にきをつける: 人にきをつけ (chars 21-27) - "watch out for people"
2. Noun + にきをつけます: 者にきをつけましょう (chars 13-23) - "be careful of pedestrians"

**Undetectable structures** (documented in test file with TODO):
- Verb[ない] + ように気をつける: Pattern matcher's wildcard mechanism doesn't reliably match the intervening tokens (ない + よう) between verb and に. Would require more sophisticated pattern matcher or separate pattern definition.

**Implementation**: Matcher in n2.rs (lines 2034-2102), tests in n2_patterns.rs (lines 4907-4972)

**Key notes**:
- Pattern structure: Noun + に(格助詞) + き(動詞, くる) + を(格助詞) + つける(動詞)
- Custom matchers: NounOrVerbMatcher (excludes よう), NiParticleMatcher, KiVerbMatcher, WoParticleMatcher, TsukeruVerbMatcher
- Kagome tokenizes 気 (attention) as き with base_form=くる (verb "to come")
- Pattern successfully matches basic noun form
- Register: standard

**Tokenization observations**:
- Noun: 人(名詞) + に(助詞/格助詞) + き(動詞, base=くる, 連用形) + を(助詞/格助詞) + つけ(動詞, base=つける)
- Verb form has intervening tokens that complicate matching: Verb + ない + よう + に + き + を + つける

**Pattern ranges**: From noun through つける verb (conjugation suffix may or may not be included)
- 人にきをつけ: chars 21-27 (without て)
- 者にきをつけましょう: chars 13-23 (includes ましょう)

**Limitations**:

- Future enhancement: Could split into two separate patterns or improve wildcard matching
